Class of 2014 Master's Degree Convocation Highlights

Published: July 15, 2014  |  Category: Alumni Events
Text Size: A- A A+
| 🖨 Print ✉ Email
Graduates from various Master's programs gathering for a group photograph following the 2014 Commencement Ceremony at the university square.

The University proudly congratulates the graduating Class of 2014. The annual commencement ceremony, held last week, marked the culmination of years of rigorous academic training, research, and dedication for our post-graduate cohorts.

This year, our diverse Master's programs saw an exceptional group of professionals and academics receive their degrees across various disciplines. The Vice-Chancellor emphasized in the opening address the critical role our graduates play in upholding societal order, ethical leadership, and innovation in their respective fields.

Following the formal ceremony in the Grand Hall, graduates, academic staff, and families gathered at the quadrangle for the traditional cohort photographs. We wish all our 2014 alumni the utmost success as they return to their professional careers or embark on new journeys.
